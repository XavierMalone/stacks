Needs a title

Barcodes that no database recognised. Their barcodes are already saved; add a title when you have a minute (cases are easy to fill in from the couch). Once named, scanning the same barcode will always match.

Backup & sync

Your collection lives on this device and works offline. Export a backup now and then, or import one on another device.

Export to Libib

Creates one CSV per Libib type using Libib's own import templates. Items with no barcode are skipped by Libib unless you use its Force Import Mode (Pro).

Lookup sources

MusicBrainz, Discogs and Open Library work out of the box with no keys.

Setup for the proxy is in README.md. It's a free 2-minute Cloudflare Worker.
